Rename a Session, choose its model, control when it sleeps, and delete or restore it. Each of these is the Session's Creator's to do: others in the Session see the result.

## Rename it

<Steps>

1. Press the pencil beside the title in the Session's header, or on its row in the sidebar.

2. Type the name, up to 80 characters, and press Enter. Esc cancels.

</Steps>

A Session without a name shows its Work branch. Clear the name to go back to it.

## Choose the model and thinking level

Two buttons under the prompt box show the Session's Model and [Thinking level](/reference/glossary/#thinking-level), such as **GPT-5.5** and **High**. The thinking button says **Thinking** while no level is set, and the Harness picks one. It's one setting for the Session.

<Steps>

1. Press the model button and pick a Model. The list is yours: the Models of your [Connected providers](/guides/connect-your-providers/), grouped by Provider, and Claude's when you have a [Claude credential](/guides/add-your-claude-credential/). In a Claude Code Session, it's Claude Code's models, and **Default** is Claude Code's own default.

2. Press the thinking button and slide to a level: **Off**, **Minimal**, **Low**, **Medium**, **High**, **Extra** or **Max**, as far as the Model takes them. With the keyboard, use the arrow keys and press Enter. **Use the default** goes back to no level.

</Steps>

A Model that takes no thinking level has no thinking button. Picking a Model that doesn't take the level set drops the level.

The change applies from the next Turn, and the transcript says who switched to what. Switch whenever you like, to any Provider's Model: the conversation carries over, tool calls and all. Others in the Session see the Model's name, not your list.

In a Claude Code Session, the Runner restarts Claude Code in the same conversation, and the next prompt waits until it's back. Restarting interrupts running subagents, so the web UI asks first while any run.

## Sleep and wake

A Session goes to sleep after 15 minutes, by default, when nothing is happening: no Turn or subagent running, no prompt queued, no open question, and nobody in it. Sleeping stops the agent and the Sandbox. The Workspace, the conversation and the Sandbox's disks are kept.

- **Sleep now**, in the header, puts it to sleep at once.
- **Your next prompt** wakes it, as does **Wake now** on its banner. The agent resumes the same conversation, with `node_modules`, caches and Docker's data as they were.

## Keep it awake

To keep a Session running while nobody is in it, such as for a dev server or a long command, turn on **Keep awake** in its header. The sidebar marks the Session with a sun for everyone.

Turn **Keep awake** off, or press **Sleep now**, to let it sleep again.

## When its Runner goes offline

A Session whose Runner is offline is Frozen. You can read it, and new prompts wait in its queue. When the Runner is back, the Session carries on, and runs the waiting prompts.

## Delete and restore it

**Delete Session**, the bin in the header, archives the Session. Its Sandbox is destroyed. Its Workspace and conversation are kept for 30 days by default, then deleted.

To restore it, press **Undo** in the toast, or **Restore** on it in **Archived Sessions**, in the sidebar. A restored Session starts Asleep. Your next prompt wakes it in a fresh Sandbox, without the old `node_modules` or Docker data.

## Purge it to free disk

A Runner running low on disk doesn't have to wait 30 days. In **Archived Sessions**, press **Purge** on an Archived Session, or tick several and press **Purge selected**. Anyone with access to the Repo can, not just the Session's Creator. The confirmation names each Session: once purged, it can't be restored, and its Workspace goes with any work it never pushed. A Runner that is offline frees the disk when it reconnects.

To see what is worth purging, the list shows how much of its Runner's disk each Archived Session takes: its directory with the Workspace, and its disk volumes, as actually used rather than the sizes they are allowed to grow to. Tick several and the list shows the total they free, and so does the confirmation. A Session whose Runner is offline shows its size as unknown, and the total says it is at least what is known.
