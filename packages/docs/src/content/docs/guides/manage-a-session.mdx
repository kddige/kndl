Rename a Session, choose its model, control when it sleeps, and delete or restore it. Each of these is the Session's Creator's to do: others in the Session see the result.

## Rename it

<Steps>

1. Choose **Rename** from the **⋯** menu by the Session's title, or press the pencil on its row in the sidebar.

2. Type the name, up to 80 characters, and press Enter. Esc cancels.

</Steps>

A Session without a name shows its Work branch. Clear the name to go back to it.

## Choose the model and thinking level

Two buttons under the prompt box show the Session's Model and [Thinking level](/reference/glossary/#thinking-level), such as **GPT-5.5** and **High**. The thinking button says **Thinking** while no level is set, and the Model uses its usual level. It's one setting for the Session.

<Steps>

1. Press the model button and pick a Model. Type to search. The list is yours, grouped by what runs each Model:
   - **Anthropic · Claude Code**: Claude Code's models, on your [Claude credential](/guides/add-your-claude-credential/). **Default · …** is Claude Code's own default.
   - **Anthropic · pi**: Claude's Models, run by pi.
   - Each of your [Connected providers](/guides/connect-your-providers/), such as **OpenAI**, run by pi.

2. Press the thinking button and slide to a level: **Off**, **Minimal**, **Low**, **Medium**, **High**, **Extra** or **Max**, as far as the Model takes them. With the keyboard, use the arrow keys and press Enter. **Use the default** goes back to no level.

</Steps>

A Model that takes no thinking level has no thinking button. Picking a Model that doesn't take the level set drops the level.

The change applies from the next Turn, and the transcript says who switched to what. Others in the Session see the Model's name, not your list.

- **Between pi's Models**, the switch happens between Turns, with nothing restarted, so the web UI doesn't ask first. The conversation carries over, tool calls and all. If pi can't run the Model, as when its Provider stopped working, the Session stays on the Model it ran on, and the transcript says why.
- **Between Claude Code's models**, the Runner restarts Claude Code in the same conversation, and the next prompt waits until it's back. Restarting interrupts running subagents, so the web UI asks first while any run.

### Switch between Claude Code and pi

A Model in the other group switches the Session's [Harness](/reference/glossary/#harness), the program that runs its agent. The web UI asks first, **Switch to pi?** or **Switch to Claude Code?**: press **Switch**.

- Once no Turn runs, the Runner stops one Harness and starts the other in the same Sandbox. The next prompt waits until it's up. Running subagents are interrupted.
- The other Harness continues in a conversation of its own. Before your next prompt, it's handed a [Handoff](/reference/glossary/#handoff): each Turn it hasn't seen, with the prompt, the tools the agent called and what it answered, shortened. If they don't all fit, the oldest are left out.
- The agent's reasoning, tool results, Attachments and subagents' own work don't carry over. The agent reads earlier Turns itself when your prompt needs them.
- Switching back resumes that Harness's earlier conversation, with a Handoff of only what happened since.
- An Asleep Session switches as it wakes.

The transcript names the Harness, such as "Alice switched to GPT-5.5 on pi".

## Sleep and wake

A Session goes to sleep after 15 minutes, by default, when nothing is happening: no Turn or subagent running, no prompt queued, no open question, and nobody in it. Sleeping stops the agent and the Sandbox. The Workspace, the conversation and the Sandbox's disks are kept.

- **Sleep now**, in the **⋯** menu by its title, puts it to sleep at once.
- **Your next prompt** wakes it, as does **Wake now** on its banner. The agent resumes the same conversation, with `node_modules`, caches and Docker's data as they were.

## Keep it awake

To keep a Session running while nobody is in it, such as for a dev server or a long command, press **Keep awake**, the sun in the prompt box's bar, or choose it from the **⋯** menu. The sidebar marks the Session with a sun for everyone.

Turn **Keep awake** off, or press **Sleep now**, to let it sleep again.

## When its Runner goes offline

A Session whose Runner is offline is Frozen. You can read it, and new prompts wait in its queue. When the Runner is back, the Session carries on, and runs the waiting prompts.

## Delete and restore it

**Delete Session**, in the **⋯** menu by the Session's title, archives the Session. Its Sandbox is destroyed. Its Workspace and conversation are kept for 30 days by default, then deleted.

To restore it, press **Undo** in the toast, or **Restore** on it in **Archived Sessions**, in the sidebar. A restored Session starts Asleep. Your next prompt wakes it in a fresh Sandbox, without the old `node_modules` or Docker data.

## Purge it to free disk

A Runner running low on disk doesn't have to wait 30 days. In **Archived Sessions**, press **Purge** on an Archived Session, or tick several and press **Purge selected**. Anyone with access to the Repo can, not just the Session's Creator. The confirmation names each Session: once purged, it can't be restored, and its Workspace goes with any work it never pushed. A Runner that is offline frees the disk when it reconnects.

To see what is worth purging, the list shows how much of its Runner's disk each Archived Session takes: its directory with the Workspace, and its disk volumes, as actually used rather than the sizes they are allowed to grow to. Tick several and the list shows the total they free, and so does the confirmation. A Session whose Runner is offline shows its size as unknown, and the total says it is at least what is known.
