Rename a Session, choose its model, control when it sleeps, and delete or restore it. Anyone with access to the Repo can do each of these, and everyone in the Session sees the result.

## Rename it

<Steps>

1. Choose **Rename** from the **⋯** menu by the Session's title, or from its row's menu in the sidebar: its **⋯**, a right-click, or **F2** on the row.

2. Type the name, up to 80 characters, and press Enter. Esc cancels.

</Steps>

A Session without a name shows its Work branch. Clear the name to go back to it.

## Choose the model and thinking level

Two buttons under the prompt box show the Session's Model and [Thinking level](/reference/glossary/#thinking-level), such as **GPT-5.5** and **High**. The thinking button says **Thinking** while no level is set, and the Model uses its usual level. It's one setting for the Session.

<Steps>

1. Press the model button and pick a Model. Type to search. The list is the Creator's Models, grouped by Provider: those of their [Connected providers](/guides/connect-your-providers/), and Claude's, on their [Claude credential](/guides/add-your-claude-credential/).

2. Press the thinking button and slide to a level: **Off**, **Minimal**, **Low**, **Medium**, **High**, **Extra** or **Max**, as far as the Model takes them. With the keyboard, use the arrow keys and press Enter. **Use the default** goes back to no level.

</Steps>

A Model that takes no thinking level has no thinking button. Picking a Model that doesn't take the level set drops the level.

The change applies from the next Turn, and the transcript says who switched to what. Nothing restarts, and the conversation carries over, tool calls and all. If the Model can't run, as when its Provider stopped working, the Session stays on the Model it ran on, and the transcript says why.

A [Thread](/guides/reply-in-a-thread/) keeps the Model it started on. Switch it from the Thread.

## Sleep and wake

A Session goes to sleep after 15 minutes, by default, when nothing is happening: no Turn running in the main conversation or a Thread, no subagent running, no prompt queued, no open question, and nobody in it. Sleeping stops the agent and the Sandbox. The Workspace, the conversation and the Sandbox's disks are kept.

- **Sleep now**, in the **⋯** menu by its title, puts it to sleep at once.
- **Your next prompt** wakes it, as does **Wake now** on its banner. The agent resumes the same conversation, with `node_modules`, caches and Docker's data as they were.

## Keep it awake

To keep a Session running while nobody is in it, such as for a dev server or a long command, press **Keep awake**, the sun in the prompt box's bar, or choose it from the **⋯** menu. The sidebar marks the Session with a sun for everyone.

Turn **Keep awake** off, or press **Sleep now**, to let it sleep again.

## When its Runner goes offline

A Session whose Runner is offline is Frozen. You can read it, and new prompts wait in its queue. When the Runner is back, the Session carries on, and runs the waiting prompts.

## Delete and restore it

**Delete Session**, in the **⋯** menu by the Session's title, archives the Session. Its Sandbox is destroyed. Its Workspace and conversation are kept for 30 days by default, then deleted.

To restore it, press **Undo** in the toast, or **Restore** on it in **Archived Sessions**, in the sidebar. Anyone with access to the Repo can. A restored Session starts Asleep. The next prompt wakes it in a fresh Sandbox, without the old `node_modules` or Docker data.

## Purge it to free disk

A Runner running low on disk doesn't have to wait 30 days. In **Archived Sessions**, choose **Purge** in an Archived Session's **⋯** menu, or tick several and press **Purge selected**. Anyone with access to the Repo can, not just the Session's Creator. The confirmation names each Session: once purged, it can't be restored, and its Workspace goes with any work it never pushed. A Runner that is offline frees the disk when it reconnects.

To see what is worth purging, the list shows how much of its Runner's disk each Archived Session takes: its directory with the Workspace, and its disk volumes, as actually used rather than the sizes they are allowed to grow to. Tick several and the list shows the total they free, and so does the confirmation. A Session whose Runner is offline shows its size as unknown, and the total says it is at least what is known.
