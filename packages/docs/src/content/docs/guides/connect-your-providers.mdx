Connect your own accounts at OpenAI, xAI, GitHub Copilot, Google and other [Providers](/reference/glossary/#provider), so the Sessions you start can run on their Models.

A Session runs only on its Creator's [Connected providers](/reference/glossary/#connected-provider). A Runner has none of its own, so until you connect one, **New Session** asks you to.

## What you need

- **An account at a Provider:** a subscription it lets you sign in with, such as ChatGPT, or an API key.
- **The Hub's secrets key**, `HUB_SECRETS_KEY`, which whoever runs the Hub sets up: see [Use variables and secrets](/guides/use-variables-and-secrets/#what-you-need).

Claude's Models run on your Claude setup token, not on a Provider you connect here: see [Add your Claude credential](/guides/add-your-claude-credential/).

## Find your Providers

Press your name at the bottom of the sidebar, then **Account**. Under **Providers**, your Connected providers come first, a row each. Below them, **Connect a Provider** lists the rest in two groups:

- **Subscriptions**: Providers you sign in to with a subscription. Most of them also take an API key.
- **API keys**: Providers that take only a key. **Show … more** lists the ones after the first six.

## Sign in with a subscription

<Steps>

1. On the Provider's line, press its sign-in button:

   | Provider        | Button                                  | Then you          |
   | --------------- | --------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
   | OpenAI          | **Sign in with ChatGPT**                | Paste the address |
   | xAI             | **Sign in with SuperGrok or X Premium** | Enter a code      |
   | GitHub Copilot  | **Sign in with GitHub Copilot**         | Enter a code      |
   | OpenRouter      | **Sign in with OpenRouter**             | Paste the address |
   | Kimi For Coding | **Sign in with Kimi Code**              | Enter a code      |
   | Meta            | **Sign in with Meta**                   | Enter a code      |

   GitHub Copilot first asks **Which GitHub is your Copilot subscription on?** Pick **GitHub.com**, or **GitHub Enterprise** and type its address, such as `company.ghe.com`.

2. Finish the sign-in, as the line shows:
   - **To enter a code**, press **Copy code**, then **Open the sign-in page**, and enter the code there. The line says **Waiting for …** until you have.
   - **To paste the address**, press **Open the sign-in page** and sign in. Your browser then lands on a page at `127.0.0.1` that may not load. That's expected: the Hub's machine is waiting for it, not yours. Copy the page's address from the address bar, paste it in **Address of the page you landed on**, and press **Continue**.

3. When the sign-in is done, the page says **Connected …**, and the Provider moves up to **Providers**.

</Steps>

**Cancel** stops a sign-in. If one fails, the line says **The sign-in failed** and why; press the sign-in button to try again.

## Paste an API key

<Steps>

1. On the Provider's line, press **Add API key**. For a Provider you can also sign in to, it's in the line's **⋯** menu.

2. Paste the key in its field, named as the Provider names it, such as **Gemini API key**, and press **Connect**.

</Steps>

The Hub asks the Provider for its Models with the key before it saves it. If the Provider rejects it, nothing is saved, and the line says `<Provider> rejected this key…`.

## Check its Models

A Provider's row shows the account you signed in as, or the key's last 4 characters, never the token or key itself, and **… Models · checked …**. A click on the row, or **Show … Models** in its **⋯** menu, lists the Models your Sessions can pick, each with its thinking levels.

The Hub asks the Provider for them when you connect it, and again when its list is a day old. Choose **Refresh …'s Models** in its **⋯** menu to ask now, such as after you change plans.

- **Couldn't check its Models** means the Provider didn't list them this time. Your Sessions keep the Models it listed before.
- **It doesn't work anymore** means the Provider refused to renew the sign-in, such as after you signed out there. Choose **Replace …** in its **⋯** menu and connect it again.

## Replace or disconnect it

In the Provider's **⋯** menu:

- **Replace …** signs in again, or takes a new key, in place of the old one.
- **Disconnect …** makes the Hub forget the tokens or key. It doesn't sign you out at the Provider or revoke the key.

Your Sessions stay awake either way. After a disconnect, a Session on that Provider's Models can't run a Turn until you connect it again or [switch its Model](/guides/manage-a-session/#choose-the-model-and-thinking-level).

## Where your accounts are used

Only the Sessions you start run on your Connected providers, with their subagents, and only you prompt them. Your tokens and keys stay on the Hub, encrypted. A Session's model calls go through the Hub's [Gateway](/concepts/security-model/#the-gateway), which adds them on the way out, so they never reach a Runner or a Sandbox.
